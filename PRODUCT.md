# Product

<!-- impeccable:product-schema 1 -->

## Platform

web

## Stack

Astro static site with TypeScript (approved in the plan). Hosting and domain undecided.

## Users

Recruiters screening candidates for software engineering internships, usually spending well under a minute per candidate, often arriving from a CV or LinkedIn link.

## Product Purpose

The personal portfolio of Fionn Mowlds, a software engineer seeking internships. Success: after about 30 seconds a recruiter remembers that Fionn ships real things, and knows how to get in touch.

## Positioning

Fionn builds and launches working products for real people, beyond coursework.

## Capabilities and Constraints

- Home page is an About Me section only. No project previews on it.
- Projects live on a separate index and one page each: StudyRightIreland, Kidscope, Leaving Cert notes, in that order.
- No contact form. Contact links sit on the About Me page and in the footer.
- Undecided: all About Me facts and project details. They are explicit placeholders until Fionn supplies them.

## Brand Commitments

Professional first: must not feel pop, flashy, generic, or dull. One personal detail is welcome (a rowing blade in navy, white and light blue), and reading speed comes first.

## Evidence on Hand

None yet. No project descriptions, metrics, screenshots, testimonials, employers, or grades have been supplied. Never invent them.

## Product Principles

- Show the work rather than claim skill.
- A recruiter should get the point without scrolling or waiting.
- Sound like a person, not a CV.
- Everything stated must be true and supplied by Fionn.
